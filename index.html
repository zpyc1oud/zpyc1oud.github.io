ACAIT 2026 Accepted · 1st Author

Beyond-Lip: Eye-Aware Talking Head Synthesis with Explicit Gaze Modeling

Pengyu Zha, Ji Xu, Weicheng Xie

Addresses the “dead-eye” artifact in audio-driven talking-head synthesis by explicitly modeling 2D gaze alongside expression, head pose, and jaw pose across motion representation, rendering, diffusion generation, and evaluation.

GErr 10.94 → 8.03 (ablation) EyeDiv +62.8% vs. FLOAT EyeReal −38.5% vs. FLOAT
Read the approach & renderer results

Concatenates DECA expression, head pose, and jaw parameters with 2D gaze angles into a frame-level motion representation. A gaze-conditioned semantic face renderer with gaze consistency loss reaches GErr 8.03, EAR 0.0317, PSNR 23.75, LPIPS 0.131, and ID 0.667 on the held-out split, while a Whisper-conditioned MotionDiT (Flow Matching) learns one-to-many audio-to-eye motion evaluated with the proposed EyeDiv and EyeReal protocols on VoxCeleb2.

Diffusion 2025.05 – Present

LightningHead: Audio-Driven Talking-Head Generation with LightningDiT

Project Lead (Undergraduate Innovation Project, 3-member team). Built a latent video diffusion prototype combining VAE latent compression, Whisper audio features, and a 12-layer DiT3D backbone (200k training steps), introducing factorized spatio-temporal self-attention that cuts multi-frame attention compute to 20.5% of standard 3D self-attention.
