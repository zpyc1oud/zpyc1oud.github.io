Beyond-Lip: Eye-Aware Talking Head Synthesis with Explicit Gaze Modeling

Pengyu Zha, Ji Xu, Weicheng Xie

Audio-driven talking-head synthesis with explicit gaze modeling. Beyond-Lip models expression, head pose, and eye movements to address the “dead-eye” artifact beyond lip synchronization.

Read the approach

A MotionDiT-based audio-to-motion pipeline is paired with a semantic face renderer. The work introduces Eye Motion Diversity (EyeDiv) and Eye Behavior Realism Distance (EyeReal) to evaluate eye behavior.
